AegisAI Company Policy

Working Hours:
Employees work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Leave Policy:
Employees receive 18 paid leaves per year.
Employees should apply for planned leave at least 3 days in advance.

Work From Home:
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Expense Policy:
Business expenses must be submitted within 7 days of the expense.
Receipts must be attached to the expense claim.

Security Policy:
Employees must not share company passwords or confidential information with unauthorized people.
